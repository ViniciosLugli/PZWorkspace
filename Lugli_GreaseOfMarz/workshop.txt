description=Unofficial fixes for the Gunworks and Guns of Marz gun stack. Not affiliated with their authors.
description=
description=[b]No dependencies.[/b] Each fix runs only when the mods it fixes are active. Load order does not matter.
description=
description=[h1]Fixes[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Faster world load.[/b] Guns of Marz adds its loot through Gunworks, which searches every loot table on each call. This does the same work with an index, checked against the original. Lugli - Fast Loading carries the same fix, so with both it runs once.
description=[*][b]Reload props with Ultimate Marksman.[/b] Ultimate Marksman skips a Gunworks reload step, so revolvers, shotguns and other guns without a magazine lose the shell holder or speedloader in your off hand. This puts it back.
description=[*][b]Reloads that stop repeating with Run and Reload.[/b] Run and Reload replaces the reload actions Gunworks uses to tell a reload's steps apart, so lever, bolt and pump guns rack and reload over and over, magazines ignore the ammo type and round count you pick, and in multiplayer a live round gets thrown out. Reloads work step by step again, and you can still reload on the move.
description=[*][b]Headhunter rifles you can load.[/b] Undead Survivor's Headhunter rifles take .308 rounds and M14 magazines, which Guns of Marz turns into its own. They now take Guns of Marz's 7.62x51 rounds and M14 magazine, only when your sandbox converts that ammo.
description=[*][b]Attachments UI without patches.[/b] The workbench now offers the Picatinny rail on Guns of Tien's guns, shows Guns of Marz and Guns of Tien pistols, revolvers and machine pistols the right way up and framed like vanilla handguns, draws each slot's line to its rail on the gun, and hides the bayonet slot it cannot fill (bayonets still attach from the right-click menu). Patches you already made keep looking the same.
description=[/list]
