title=Lugli - Achievements
description=Build 42 has no achievements. This adds 280+ of them, earned by what your survivor actually does: a weapon you finally used, an animal you finally handled, a way you finally died.
description=
description=[b]It changes nothing about how the game plays.[/b] No items, XP, loot or recipes. It watches, counts and tells you.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ViniciosLugli/PZWorkspace/main/Lugli_Achievements/art/ach-categories.png[/img]
description=
description=[h1]Needs [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3508537032]NeatUI Framework[/url][/h1]
description=Build 42.20 or later. NeatUI draws the window and the unlock pop-ups. The rest is pure Lua.
description=
description=[h1]13 categories[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Waking Up:[/b] the first time you do each ordinary thing
description=[*][b]Body Count:[/b] kills, by weapon and by how much noise you made
description=[*][b]Still Breathing:[/b] days survived
description=[*][b]Autodidact:[/b] skills, perks and the books behind them
description=[*][b]Nailed It:[/b] what you built, and what you set on fire
description=[*][b]Homestead:[/b] crops and animals
description=[*][b]Field Medicine:[/b] bandages, stitches and splints
description=[*][b]Roadside Attractions:[/b] driving, and where the road ends
description=[*][b]House Proud:[/b] burials, hoarding and a home worth keeping
description=[*][b]This Is How You Died:[/b] the ways it ends
description=[*][b]Secrets:[/b] hidden until you trip over them
description=[*][b]Challenges:[/b] one-off feats, like killing one while on fire and living an hour more
description=[*][b]Milestones:[/b] finishing a whole category
description=[/list]
description=[h1]Make it yours[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Per world or per character.[/b] By default every survivor in a save shares one collection, so dying does not reset it. Switch to per character in Mod Options. It applies on the next world load and deletes nothing.
description=[*][b]Your unlock sound.[/b] In the window's Settings: seven sounds or off, with its own volume. It follows you to every world and server.
description=[/list]
description=[h1]FAQ[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]How do I open it?[/b] The medal icon at the bottom of the left sidebar, or [b]F7[/b] (rebindable). Hide the sidebar button in Mod Options.
description=[*][b]Can I add it to a running save?[/b] Yes. What the game already tracks, like days survived and known recipes, counts at once. Kills and builds start from zero.
description=[*][b]Is it safe to remove?[/b] Yes. The save loads normally, and reinstalling brings your progress back.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer?[/b] Yes, and your collection is your own. The server needs the mod too: it keeps each player's progress.
description=[*][b]Languages?[/b] Ten, all complete: English, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Polish, Japanese and Korean. Names were adapted, not machine translated.
description=[*][b]Will 280+ achievements slow the game?[/b] No. A stat change only wakes the few achievements watching that stat.
description=[*][b]Can my mod add achievements?[/b] Yes. One table per achievement, and it tracks, saves and announces itself. Example on GitHub.
description=[/list]
