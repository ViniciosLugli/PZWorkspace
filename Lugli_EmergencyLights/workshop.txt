description=[h1]How they work[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Crack it once.[/b] There is no off switch, as in real life. You choose when, and where you leave it.
description=[*][b]Drop it or throw it.[/b] It lands where you aim and keeps burning. Light a corridor before you walk in.
description=[*][b]No alarm noise.[/b] A vanilla thrown item is as loud as a house alarm; these land with a small sound. A burning flare and the flare gun do draw zombies, so pick your spot.
description=[*][b]Watch it die.[/b] It dims as it burns down, then goes out and leaves a spent one behind.
description=[/list]
description=[h1]Make your own flares[/h1]
description=Flares can be crafted; glow sticks cannot. Each recipe needs a crafting surface and is learned from the Roadside Pyrotechnics Handbook. Without the book, you learn the flares at Reloading 4 and the cartridge at Reloading 6.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Road Flare[/b], Reloading 2: gunpowder, fertiliser, paper, duct tape, matches, and a knife you keep
description=[*][b]Repack a Burned Road Flare[/b], Reloading 1: the burned flare, gunpowder, fertiliser, matches
description=[*][b]Flare Cartridge[/b], Reloading 3: a road flare, aluminium, gunpowder
description=[/list]
description=[h1]FAQ[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Can I add it to a running save?[/b] Yes. The items appear in containers the game has not generated yet.
description=[*][b]Is it safe to remove?[/b] Yes. Items you carry go with the mod, as with any item mod.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer?[/b] Yes, dedicated servers too. The server keeps every light, so all players see it in the same place, and it survives saves and reloads.
description=[*][b]Languages?[/b] English and Brazilian Portuguese.
description=[/list]
