title=Lugli - Emergency Lights
description=It is dark, you are somewhere you do not know, and every light you own has to be carried in the hand you would rather be fighting with.
description=
description=Build 42 has no glow sticks, no chem lights and no flares. It also has a matching hole underneath: a lit item on the ground emits no light at all, which is why vanilla puts a lamp out [i]before[/i] it lands.
description=
description=[b]These do not go out.[/b] Crack one and it burns wherever you leave it.
description=

description=[b]This is not optional.[/b] The light comes from an engine call plain Lua cannot reach, and ZombieBuddy is what lets this mod reach it. Subscribe to both and enable both, or the sticks you crack will not light anything. Load order does not matter.

description=
description=For scale: the game caps its own street lamps at 20 tiles and always has. A road flare lights three quarters of that from your hand, and the flare gun goes twice past it. Getting past that cap is exactly what ZombieBuddy is here for.
description=
description=[b]Every duration is in-game time[/b], so it scales with your day length setting: four hours of glow stick is four hours of your survivor's day, not of yours. Every number above is a sandbox slider, and these are the defaults rather than the limits.
description=
description=[h1]What that lets you do[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Crack it once.[/b] There is no off switch, because there is not one in real life either. What you choose is when, and where you leave it.
description=[*][b]Leave one behind.[/b] Drop it at the doorway you mean to come back through. Walk away, come back later, it is still burning.
description=[*][b]Throw it.[/b] Lit sticks use the game's own throw animation and aiming, and land where you aimed. Light a corridor before you walk into it.
description=[*][b]Be quiet about it.[/b] The engine's thrown object makes a noise identical to a ringing house alarm. These are silent by default, and exactly as loud as you choose to make them.
description=[*][b]Watch it die.[/b] The light dims as it burns down, then goes out and leaves a spent stick behind. A burnt road flare leaves a burnt road flare, not a glow stick.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Then tune all of it[/h1]
description=Every one of these is a sandbox option on the New Game screen, saved into your preset like any vanilla setting:
description=[list]
description=[*]Burn time and light radius, per family
description=[*]Throw range, and how much noise any of it makes
description=[*]The flare's wash across the sky, and the map marker
description=[*]Loot rarity
description=[*]The light scan, if you would rather have the frame time back
description=[/list]

description=[h1]FAQ[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Can I add it to a save I already started?[/b] Yes. The items appear in containers the game has not generated yet, so buildings you have already stripped stay stripped.
description=
description=[b]Is it safe to remove?[/b] Yes. Remove it and the save loads normally. Anything you were carrying goes with the mod, as with any item mod.
description=
description=[b]Does it work in multiplayer?[/b] Yes. A lit item is a swapped item plus an absolute timestamp, so it reads the same for everyone and survives saves, reloads and containers.
description=
description=[b]And on a dedicated server?[/b] Fine. The items and their timers live server side; the light is drawn on each client.
description=
description=[b]Which languages?[/b] English and Brazilian Portuguese, both complete.
description=
description=[b]ZombieBuddy asks me to approve a Java mod.[/b] Expected. Approve it and enable the mod. ZombieBuddy never runs Java code you have not agreed to, and asks once.
