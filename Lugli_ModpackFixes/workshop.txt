title=Lugli - B42 Modpack Compats & Fixes
description=Fixes for conflicts between popular Build 42 mods, and bugs inside some of them.
description=
description=[b]No dependencies.[/b] Each mod fix turns on only with the mods it fixes. If an update changes their code, it stands down instead of guessing. Each fix has a sandbox switch.
description=
description=[h1]Combat and zombies[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3833349401]Super Gore[/url]:[/b] zombies it finishes off (a severed head, a burst chest) now count as your kills.
description=[*][b]Super Gore + [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3783283328]Special Zombies: Tank[/url]:[/b] a Tank no longer dies to almost any hit, or heals when a limb is cut.
description=[*][b]Vanilla:[/b] wearing an ALICE belt or chest rig, reloads without an ammo strap and filling magazines no longer error. This makes [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3837229266]Reload Those Weapons Again[/url] redundant.
description=[/list]
description=[h1]Hotbar and keys[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2366717227]SwapIt[/url] + [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3771638611]Fancy Handwork[/url]:[/b] modifier + hotbar key takes the item into your off hand again.
description=[*][b]SwapIt + [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3737864976]H.E.C.U.[/url]:[/b] the hotbar key puts helmets and masks on and off again.
description=[*][b]SwapIt + [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3696291148]Better Pause[/url]:[/b] hotbar keys work while paused again.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3771638611]Fancy Handwork B42.20[/url]:[/b] the swap-hands keys work, and key presses stop raising errors.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3775549570]Alice's Weapon Sling[/url]:[/b] cancelling an attach no longer leaves a ghost weapon on your body.
description=[/list]
description=[h1]Inventory and menus[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3758971325]Better Item Info[/url] + [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3722134990]Guns of Marz[/url]:[/b] tooltip rows from other mods (backpack upgrades, seed seasons) stay visible.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3830103302]Better Item Transfer[/url] + [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3422220305]Picking Meister[/url]:[/b] Grab and Unpack Contents is back beside Drop and Place.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2996978365]Dynamic Backpack Upgrades[/url]:[/b] upgraded bags keep their capacity on a server after a restart, and bags past the 50 limit stop asking to be fixed.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3437629766]CleanUI[/url]:[/b] the vanilla tutorial no longer errors on its container steps, and the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3816324722]Attachments UI[/url] workbench takes CleanUI's look.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3781990933]Clear Driver View Fix[/url] + [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3501302358]Vehicle Seat UI (KI5)[/url]:[/b] the seat diagram no longer flashes when you start driving.
description=[/list]
description=[h1]Items, loot and world[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2713921292]Undead Survivor[/url]:[/b] gas masks are kept when you remove the filter, the Stalker knife can be sharpened, and in multiplayer a corpse shows only loot you can take.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3745718141]Explosives[/url]:[/b] in multiplayer the grenadier zombies' full grenade bandolier really holds its grenades.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820938264]'67 Chevy Impala[/url]:[/b] the Impala spawns in the parking lots, traffic jams and junkyards its mod lists it in.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3171167894]damnlib[/url]:[/b] the seats of its military vehicles can be repaired with duct tape or needle and thread.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3457132019]Specific Loot (KI5)[/url]:[/b] burnt wrecks, F350/F450 work trucks, the Power Wagon PA and the Type 2 Van Apocalypse spawn with loot, and no loot gets stuck in the sealed seats of the LAV-300 or the Step Van SWAT, so you can sit in them again.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3783433804]Nellis Air Force Base[/url]:[/b] in multiplayer its interiors stay powered, a generator carried in no longer refills for free, and you always have a way out, even after a relog or a restart.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3436499337]Vehicle Military Zones[/url] + [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3624971238]Yet Another PZ Library[/url]:[/b] its old zone script no longer errors on world load.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3413150945]More Damaged Objects[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3484263516]Raven Creek[/url]:[/b] street lights, hedges, ladders, fences and signs get their vanilla properties back.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3342191739]Gyde's Trait Magazines[/url] + [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3776852122]Extended[/url]:[/b] the Handy magazine gives its four skill levels.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2447729538]Fluffy Hair[/url]:[/b] two hoodies no longer show the hood up when it is down.
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3736837081]Get Up Quick[/url]:[/b] what you hold is hidden again while you stand up from the ground, as in vanilla.
description=[/list]
description=[h1]Generators and power[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3794099205]Nick's Generator Manager[/url]:[/b] its once-a-minute billing no longer freezes the game per running generator. The Info window and tooltip print its numbers like the game's own, and a [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3804093735]Badlands Power[/url] battery bank lists only what it pays for. In multiplayer, only a player in range and allowed in its safehouse can change what it powers.
description=[*][b]Nick's Generator Manager + [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3723726293]Neat Rocco's UI[/url]:[/b] the generator window lists what that generator pays for, so it adds up to its Total. Manage Power opens in Neat Rocco's style and no longer stutters while open.
description=[*][b]Badlands Power + Neat Rocco's UI:[/b] the generator window keeps Badlands Power's names, and the battery bank shows its charge and batteries instead of a fuel tank.
description=[/list]
description=[h1]Questions[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Load order?[/b] Anywhere after the mods it fixes.
description=[*][b]Add to or remove from a running save?[/b] Yes, it adds no items or objects.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer?[/b] Install it on the server and on every client.
description=[*][b]Using Guns of Marz?[/b] Add [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3839186582]Grease of Marz[/url] for its gun fixes.
description=[*][b]Server admins:[/b] create [i]Lua/serverprof-on.txt[/i] in the server's cache folder to log what each mod sends to players in [i]Lua/serverprof-sends.txt[/i].
description=[*][b]Did it load?[/b] A [i][Lugli/ModpackFixes][/i] line in console.txt lists each fix and if it loaded.
description=[/list]
