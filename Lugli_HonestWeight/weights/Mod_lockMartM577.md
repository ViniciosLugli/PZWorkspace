# Lock-Mart Industries M577

Weights Lugli - Honest Weight sets for [Lock-Mart Industries M577](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3652371248): 8 items. [All sources](../WEIGHTS.md)

**Game** is the weight without Honest Weight. **Honest** is the weight at the default options (Realism 100%, Bulk floor Normal, Scale 1.5), in the game's own units. A container that holds a liquid or is used up shows its empty weight in brackets.

Vehicle parts (*) stay in real kilograms: their weight is also the car's driving mass, so Scale does not apply to them.
