description=[h1]What it does[/h1]
description=[b]Wind gate.[/b] The game redraws every plant that can sway on every frame, even while it stands still. While the wind leaves them at rest, this draws them with the rest of the still scenery and hands them back the moment they move. The sway itself is untouched. It helps most zoomed out in calm or light wind, and stands aside in strong wind.
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ViniciosLugli/PZWorkspace/main/Lugli_Optimizations/art/fix-windgate.gif[/img]
description=
description=[b]Chunk height scan.[/b] When new ground loads, the engine reads 130,321 entries where only 361 can hold anything. This reads just those 361 (on a 1080p or larger screen).
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ViniciosLugli/PZWorkspace/main/Lugli_Optimizations/art/fix-zextents.gif[/img]
description=
description=[b]Room tile index.[/b] Adding a tile to a room no longer checks every tile already in it. Both of these give the same answer for far less work, but are too small to feel on their own.
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ViniciosLugli/PZWorkspace/main/Lugli_Optimizations/art/fix-roomindex.gif[/img]
description=
description=Every part can be switched off on its own. Most ideas tried showed no real gain in the running game and were dropped, which is why the list is short.
description=
description=[h1]FAQ[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]ZombieBuddy asks me to approve a Java mod.[/b] Expected. Approve it once and enable the mod.
description=[*][b]Is it signed?[/b] Yes, and ZombieBuddy's author list names me. A signature proves who built it, not what it does, so the full source is on GitHub.
description=[*][b]Why no fps number?[/b] Average fps hides the frames you feel. Time per frame is what was measured.
description=[*][b]Other optimization mods?[/b] Fine unless they patch the same engine code: then whichever loads last wins, silently. If something looks wrong with both on, turn one off before reporting it.
description=[*][b]Dedicated server?[/b] Not needed. It is client side.
description=[*][b]Add or remove it mid-save?[/b] Yes. It writes nothing to your save or your game folder.
description=[*][b]Something looks wrong.[/b] Add [i]-Dlugli.opt.windgate=off[/i] to the [b]vmArgs[/b] list in ProjectZomboid64.json: the wind gate is the only part that changes how anything is drawn. If that fixes it, open an issue on GitHub.
description=[/list]
